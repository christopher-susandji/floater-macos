Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Floater does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies are used.
  • No usage or diagnostic data is sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • Timers and settings are stored locally on your Mac and are removed when you delete the app.

Camera access

Floater can act as a virtual camera so a timer can be used as a live video source in apps such as Keynote. This feature is optional and runs entirely on your device. No video or audio is recorded, transmitted, or shared.

Contact
